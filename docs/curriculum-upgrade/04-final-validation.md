OBJECTIVE

Determine whether the implemented course meets its stated learning outcomes and fix actionable defects. Do not certify unproven learning effectiveness.

AUDIT THE COMPLETE LEARNING JOURNEY

Check:
- A new learner can progress from rules to complete games.
- Opening principles connect to middlegame plans.
- Tactical training includes both recognition and creation of opportunities.
- Defence extends beyond stopping immediate mate.
- Endgames include conversion and appropriate drawing tasks.
- Both colours receive meaningful coverage.
- Higher levels require unfamiliar positions, branching decisions, counterplay, and sustained play.
- Independent assessments do not leak solutions through titles, prompts, hints, or reused practice positions.
- Completion, mastery, and practical application remain distinct.
- Jump-ahead scoring and competency minimums work as specified.
- Existing progress migrates safely.
- Draft or unverified content is excluded from the release path.

TEST PRACTICAL ASSESSMENTS

Use representative learner behaviours:
- Memorizes puzzle answers but cannot form a plan.
- Finds tactics but neglects defence.
- Knows an opening line but fails when the opponent deviates.
- Chooses a sound alternative to the engine's top move.
- Preserves a good position using a different valid plan.
- Wins material but fails to convert.
- Uses hints or repeated attempts to reach completion.
- Returns after an extended break.

Verify the system distinguishes these cases where its assessment claims require it.

REVIEW SCORING

Check for:
- Single-line answer bias.
- Excessive reliance on exact engine moves or scores.
- False success caused by weak opponent play.
- False failure for reasonable alternatives.
- Winning/drawing claims beyond the search or rules implementation.
- Scores inflated by XP, retries, hints, or repeated items.

DELIVERABLES

- Fix defects and rerun relevant checks.
- Produce a coverage matrix connecting outcomes to teaching, practice, assessment, and practical evidence.
- Produce a release-readiness report with verified strengths, defects, limitations, and unresolved blockers.
- Identify which learning-effectiveness claims still require testing with real learners.
- Provide a concrete usability/pilot-study plan and metrics for later evaluation.
- Update learner-facing descriptions to match demonstrated capabilities. Do not imply an official Master title or an unvalidated rating.
- Produce a release candidate and rollback/migration notes.

Do not fabricate user-study results or claim automated tests prove teaching effectiveness.

Do not merge, publish, or deploy. Finish with the release candidate status, verification evidence, blockers, and exact remaining steps.
