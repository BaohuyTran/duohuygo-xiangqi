## 2. What may be used, and how — Proposal

### 2.1 Positions (every exercise, check, placement and exam item)

Allowed sources, recorded per item in `source.kind`:

| Kind | Definition | Required verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `generated` | Produced by an in-repo, seeded generator script (as `scripts/generate-puzzles.ts`), kept only when the claimed property is proven | Generator + seed + script version recorded; the content validator re-proves the claim |
| `authored` | Constructed by a course author for the item, not transcribed from any publication | Full validator; the author states the idea; independent review (§6) |
| `derived` | Obtained from an allowed item by a recorded transformation: colour swap, file mirror, position after a stated move sequence, removal/addition of named pieces | `derivedFrom` + transformation recorded; re-verified as a new item (a transformation does not carry a proof over automatically) |
| `course-game` | A position from a game played for the course (§3) | Game record id + move number; the game replays legally from `START_FEN` |
| `legacy` | One of today's 195 lesson positions or 60 puzzles, reused per 05-migration.md | `legacyRef`; re-verified by the new validators |

**Not allowed:** transcribing positions, problems, studies or puzzle sets from books, magazines, websites, apps,
databases, engine test suites or other courses — including "lightly changed" copies. If an author notices that a
generated or authored position matches a published one, it is replaced (not cited), because the course cannot prove
it is original.

### 2.2 Knowledge that may come from references

| Material | Allowed use | Citation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rules** (moves, check, results, repetition rules) | Restated in our own words; the course's reference is the AXF rules (03 §8.1); every rules sentence in b05l3 and e07l3 is checked against the rulebook | The rulebook edition and article, in the item's `source.refs` and in a course reference list (§5.3) — edition Unresolved (03 UA3) |
| **Opening names and plans** (Central Cannon, Screen Horses, …; 02 §4) | Names, short move sequences that are common knowledge, and general plans, written in our own words and with the purpose of each move explained; every sequence legality-checked from `START_FEN` | Claims of the kind "this is the main line", "the most popular reply", "theory considers this equal" need a cited reference **or** are phrased as the course's choice ("the course teaches …") |
| **Pattern and piece names, conventional values** | Common knowledge; names in en + vi per the glossary (02 §4) | None needed; the glossary records the chosen names and their Vietnamese/Chinese forms |
| **Endgame theory** ("this material wins/draws") | Only with a cited, reviewed source **or** proven for the exact position (02 i10, a09) | Source in `source.refs`; draws are never claimed from search alone (03 §8, 02 e07) |
| **Annotations, explanations, tables, diagrams** of any publication | Not copied or translated; we write our own explanations from our own analysis | — |

Citation format (Proposal): `{ "title": …, "author": …, "edition": …, "year": …, "locator": "art. 23" | "p. 41",
"checkedBy": <reviewer role>, "checkedAt": <date> }`. A citation is added only after the reviewer has opened the
source and confirmed the locator. **AI-assisted authoring** (the course is written with AI agents): no citation,
quotation, game, player name or historical claim may be produced from model memory; anything not checked against an
opened source is removed. This is the main fabrication risk of the project.

## 3. Annotated games (02 U1: a10, m08, e09 examples, reviews) — Proposal

### 3.1 Options and risks

| Option | Benefits | Risks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Course games**: games played for the course by authors/testers against each other or against the training partners, annotated by the course | Fully owned; positions, moves and annotations are original; can be designed to show the plans the lesson teaches (opening plan → target → transition → endgame) | Quality: partner games are weak (04 §5.5); human author games need a willing player of adequate level; must not be presented as "master games" |
| **B. Historical games, moves only, with citation** | Real, purposeful play; cultural value | Legal status of game scores and of database compilations varies by country (e.g. database rights) — **Unresolved, owner decision**; transcription errors are common (a move list must replay legally); personal data of living players; risk of misattribution; annotations of the source cannot be used |
| **C. Classical manuals** (works old enough to be in the public domain) | Public-domain source text likely | Positions are often composed rather than played; old notation is ambiguous; editions with modern commentary are not public domain; still needs every claim checked |

### 3.2 Policy

1. **Pilot and first release: option A only.** Games are played for the course, recorded with a game record id,
   replayed legally from `START_FEN` by the engine, safety-reviewed (post-game review of 04 §5.6), and annotated in
   our own words. Partner self-play is allowed only as raw material that an author then curates; lessons say
   "a course game", never "a master game".
2. **Option B** becomes available only after the owner decides the legal question (S07-U1). If approved, each game
   needs: players, event, year and at least two independent sources whose move lists agree; moves only (no copied
   annotations); legal replay from `START_FEN` (an illegal or ambiguous move rejects the game); only players who are
   deceased or whose games were published at official public events (owner to confirm); the citation in the item.
3. **Option C** under the same conditions as B, citing the edition used.
4. Every annotated-game claim ("the losing move", "the turning point") must be supported by the post-game review or
   by an authoring-time search, with the limits stated (03 §11; 04 §5.4 "what cannot be judged").
5. **Learner games** stay on the learner's device (and account, if signed in) and are never turned into course
   content.

## 4. Engines and tools — Proposal (licence facts Verified in 04 §6)

1. **In-repo engine and solver** (`src/engine`, own code): the only engine shipped. Forced-outcome claims are proven
   by the exhaustive solver (`forcedWinMoves`/`hasForcedWin`), for **every** defence, not only the authored ones.
2. **External engines** (Fairy-Stockfish, Pikafish: GPL-3.0) are used **only at authoring time**, installed outside
   the repository (like the global Playwright used by `ui:check`), never vendored, never bundled, never downloaded by
   the app. Their output is stored as evidence data in the review record (§5): engine, version, options (variant,
   threads, network), command (`go depth N` / `go mate N`), result, date. Shipping any engine in the app needs the
   owner's licence decision first (04 R4).
3. **Engine output is evidence, not verification.** A strong engine's first choice is not proof (04 §2.4); a search
   score is never the answer key of a strategic item (02 §5.3) and feedback never says "the engine says".
4. **Uniqueness corpus.** New assessment items (checks, placement, exams) are compared, in normalised form including
   file mirrors and colour swaps (03 §4.2 rule 4), against every lesson position, pool item, learn card, puzzle and
   other pool. The §9 command 4 of 05-migration.md is the prototype (today: 7 learn-card/item pairs, no other
   duplicates).

### 4.1 Verification by claim type

| Claim | How it is verified | Stated limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Position is legal | Validator: side not to move not in check, mover has a legal move, **piece counts and reachable points** (extend the puzzle rules to lessons; fixes the 12 `POS` positions) | Legal ≠ reachable from the start position; content says "position", not "game position", unless it is a course or cited game replayed from `START_FEN` (04 §2.1) |
| Move is legal / line legal | Engine `legalMoves` along the stored line | — |
| Forced mate / forced win of material | Exhaustive solver for every defence; material claims by the stated search depth and threshold (`WIN_MATERIAL_THRESHOLD`, `src/puzzles/logic.ts:19-21`) | Within the stated horizon only |
| Accepted alternatives | Complete move table per decision point (04 §5.3), each entry `computed` or `authored`, validator recomputes `computed` entries | Strategic classes are reviewed judgements, not proofs |
| Opponent replies in `line` items are not blunders | Safety guard on each authored reply (04 §5.2) | Shallow horizon |
| Endgame results | Exact-position proof or a cited source | No "always wins/draws" from search; no draw claims from search |
| Rules statements | Rulebook citation, reviewer check | Training ruleset differences disclosed (03 §8.3) |
| Names, notation, translations | Glossary + bilingual review (en and vi carry the same content) | — |
| Practical-task fairness | 03 §5.7 guards (N1/N2 rates, narrow positions) | Pilot-calibrated thresholds |

## 5. Metadata and review record — Proposal

### 5.1 Per item (in the content JSON)

Required on every exercise, check/placement/exam item and practical task (03 §4.2 rule 9 lists most of them):
`id`, `version`, `status`, `source` (`kind`, `generator`/`seed`, `derivedFrom`, `legacyRef`, `gameId`, `refs`),
strand, colour, `tactic`/`quiet`, `critical`, misconception ids.

### 5.2 Review records

- **Location:** `src/content/reviews/<unitId>.json` (one file per unit; puzzles in `src/content/reviews/puzzles.json`).
  Not imported by app code, so not bundled (Vite bundles only imported modules; the loaders glob only
  `lessons/*.json` and `puzzles/*.json`, Verified `src/content/index.ts:4-8`, `src/puzzles/catalog.ts:4`). Read by
  the content tests.
- **Format:**
  ```json
  {
    "id": "b05l2-04",
    "version": 2,
    "contentHash": "sha256 of the normalised item JSON + its en and vi texts",
    "author": "content-author (agent session or initials)",
    "checks": [
      { "name": "validator", "tool": "src/content/validate.ts", "result": "pass", "at": "2026-11-02" },
      { "name": "forced-mate-all-defences", "tool": "forcedWinMoves depth 3", "result": "pass", "at": "2026-11-02" },
      { "name": "cross-check", "tool": "Fairy-Stockfish 1.1.12, go mate 3", "result": "agrees", "at": "2026-11-02" }
    ],
    "reviews": [
      { "role": "content-reviewer", "by": "agent session or initials", "at": "2026-11-03", "verdict": "approve",
        "scope": ["chess", "en", "vi"], "notes": "…" }
    ]
  }
  ```
- **Binding to stable ids:** a record applies to one `id` + `version` + `contentHash`. The content test recomputes the
  hash; any change to the position, answer table, prompt or explanation (either language) invalidates the record and
  the item falls back to `draft` for release purposes until re-reviewed.
- **Independence:** `reviews[].by` must differ from `author` for chess content and assessment logic (PLAN.md: an
  author's own review does not replace independent review); the test enforces it.
- **No personal data:** role names, agent session ids or initials only; no email addresses.

### 5.3 Course reference list

`docs/curriculum-upgrade/references.md` (to be created by the first item that cites something): every cited work
once, with the citation fields of §2.2; items refer to it by key.

## 6. Draft status and the release path — Proposal

- **Statuses:** `draft` → `verified` (validators and proofs pass) → `reviewed` (independent approval recorded,
  hash matches) → `published`; `retired` (id reserved, never served).
- **Drafts never reach learners:** draft items live in `src/content/drafts/` (not matched by the loaders' glob
  patterns), or carry `status` below `published`; a content test fails the build if any loaded item is not
  `published` or lacks a matching review record. Check, placement and exam pools additionally refuse anything
  unreviewed (03 §4.2 rule 9). Practice items follow the same rule (no "practice exceptions").
- Pool sizes (03 §4.3) are counted over `published` items only, so a pool short of reviewed items makes the check
  unavailable with a message, not padded with drafts.
- Reused legacy items enter as `verified` (they pass today's validators) and become `published` only after the
  changes listed for them in 05-migration.md §4 and review.

## 7. Content versioning — Proposal

- **Item `version`** (02 §3.3): incremented on a **substantive** change — position, side, accepted-move table or
  classes, solution, goal, prompt meaning, scoring, explanation meaning. Credit and grants record the version
  answered and are not invalidated by a new version (03 §3.3). Editorial fixes (typos, wording with the same
  meaning) keep the version but change the hash, so they still need a (lighter) re-review.
- **Content set version:** `CONTENT_VERSION` per release, recorded in grants where 05-migration.md §8.3 asks for
  `contentVersion`/`assessmentVersion`.
- **Legacy map version:** `LEGACY_MAP_VERSION` (05-migration.md §8.4).
- **Generators:** a changed generator gets a new version string; regenerated items get new ids unless they are
  byte-identical.

## 8. Ids are never reused — Proposal (motivated by Verified history)

The basics rewrite of 2026-10-21 reused lesson ids for different content without a migration (05-migration.md §6).
Policy: ids are never reused for different content; retired ids (old lesson ids `u1l1`–`u15l3`, the orphans
`u2l3`, `u3l3`, `u4l3`, `u5l*`, `u6l*`, unit ids `u1`–`u15`, and every retired new id) are listed in a
`src/content/retired-ids.json` that the validator checks new ids against.

## 9. Licensing of the course — Unresolved

- The repository has no licence (§1); code and content are therefore "all rights reserved" by default. The owner
  decides the licence of code and of content (they may differ) — S07-U2.
- Contributors (agents and humans) author original material for the repository; external contributors would need
  an agreement once a licence exists (owner).
- Any third-party material (should the owner ever allow it, e.g. option B games, a GPL engine) must carry its
  licence/attribution text and must be compatible with the chosen licence (04 §6.3).

## 10. Checklists — Proposal

**Before an item is `verified`:** source recorded and allowed (§2.1); validator incl. reachable points; claimed
outcome proven for every defence; complete move table where required; uniqueness corpus check for assessment
items; en and vi texts present with matching placeholders.

**Before `reviewed`:** independent content reviewer approves chess accuracy, terminology and the explanation
(both languages); citations opened and confirmed; no copied text; limits of any engine-based claim stated in the
feedback where relevant; review record written with the current hash.

`course-game`, `legacy`); **SD2** course games only for annotated games until the owner decides on historical games;

bound to id + version + hash, author ≠ reviewer; **SD5** drafts outside the loaders and a release test; **SD6** ids
