OBJECTIVE

Deliver complete, playable, independently reviewed units that advance the approved learning outcomes.

Do not produce a large volume of shallow lessons merely to satisfy a chapter count.

FOR EACH UNIT

Implement:
- The approved lesson sequence and prerequisites.
- Concise explanations with interactive examples.
- Contrasting examples showing when the idea fails.
- Guided exercises with gradually reduced assistance.
- Independent exercises using unfamiliar positions.
- Relevant training from both colours and both sides of the idea.
- A practical continuation or game task appropriate to the objective.
- Position-specific feedback and remediation.
- Independent assessment items separate from practice.
- English and Vietnamese content.
- Stable identifiers, competency tags, source metadata, and content versioning.

QUALITY RULES

- Do not reveal the motif in independent assessment prompts.
- Do not label a position “realistic” merely because many pieces are present.
- Do not create duplicate exercises by changing irrelevant pieces or mirroring without adding learning value.
- Do not teach opening moves without their purpose, common alternatives, and middlegame connection.
- Do not present all sacrifices as correct or all exchanges as desirable.
- Include decisions to defend, consolidate, improve a piece, simplify, or reject an unsound attack.
- Endgame claims must be verified for the exact position and applicable ruleset.
- Ensure practical tasks actually require the intended skill.
- Use original explanations. Respect source licenses and attribution requirements.
- If reviewers cannot establish a claim, revise, downgrade the claim, or exclude the item.

VALIDATION

For each exercise, verify as applicable:
1. Position legality and side to move.
2. Legal authored moves.
3. Objective correctness and appropriate defensive responses.
4. Alternative valid learner moves.
5. Failure cases and feedback accuracy.
6. Consistency between board, prompt, hints, and explanation.
7. Difficulty and prerequisites.
8. Whether success measures understanding or merely recognition of a disclosed pattern.

For practical continuations, verify the opponent can challenge the learner's plan and that scoring tolerates reasonable alternatives.

Keep a review record tied to stable content IDs. Mark unverified material as draft and keep it out of the published learning path.

INTEGRATION

- Update ordering, prerequisites, assessment coverage, migration maps, and backlog.
- Preserve existing progress and avoid falsely awarding new competence.
- Run relevant automated content checks, tests, and build checks.
- Perform a representative UI walkthrough, including both colours.
- Independent reviewers must recheck substantive fixes.

DEFINITION OF DONE

All selected units are playable, reviewed, validated, translated, integrated, and documented. No placeholders are counted as completed curriculum.

Do not publish, merge, or deploy.

Report:
- Delivered units and learning outcomes.
- Content inventory by exercise type and competency.
- Review and verification results.
- Remaining uncertainties.
- The next dependency-ready batch.
